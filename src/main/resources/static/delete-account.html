Delete your Spot it account

Spot it by Spot It International

You can delete your Spot it account and all data linked to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open Spot it and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You are signed out on all devices straight away.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email spotitinternational@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Spot it account". We will confirm and process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details: name, email address, password and date of birth
  • All cycle and health logs: period dates, flow, mood, symptoms, intimacy entries and notes
  • Your cycle settings, goal and reminder preferences
  • SpotPoints, streaks, badges, challenge progress and reward redemptions
  • Subscription records, device records and data-export records

How long it takes

Your account is scheduled for deletion as soon as you request it. After a 30-day grace period, all of the data listed above is permanently erased and cannot be recovered. If you requested deletion by mistake, contact us within those 30 days and we can cancel it. We do not keep any of your personal data after that period.

Delete your data but keep your account

If you only want to erase your logs, go to Settings and tap Reset all data. This permanently deletes your logs, SpotPoints and settings and keeps your login.
