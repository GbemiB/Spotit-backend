Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spot It International ("Spot it", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and deletes information when you use the Spot it mobile app (package name com.spotit.app) and its related services. Spot it is a menstrual cycle tracking app. Because cycle information is health information, we collect only what the app needs to work, and we never sell it.

Spot it is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Cycle, period and ovulation predictions are estimates based on what you enter. They must not be used as contraception or to diagnose a condition. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns.

1. Information we collect

We collect only information you give us directly and a small amount of technical information needed to run your account.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or files.
  • We do not collect your device advertising ID.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: cycle tracking, predictions, insights, reminders and rewards.
  • To create, secure and maintain your account, including sending one-time verification codes by email.
  • To respond to your requests, including data export and account deletion.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not use your health information for advertising, and we do not use it to make decisions about you outside the app.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your health information with advertisers, data brokers, employers or insurers.

We use a small number of service providers who process data only on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Cloud hosting and database (Render, servers located in the United States) to store your account and logs.
  • Email delivery to send verification codes, password-reset codes and your data export to the email address on your account.

We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so by a valid and binding legal request, or to protect the rights and safety of our users. If that happens we will disclose only what is strictly required.

5. How we protect your information

  • All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes.
  • Access to your data requires your signed-in session, and you can sign out at any time.

No system is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your device locked.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is scheduled for deletion and you are signed out on all devices. After a 30-day grace period, your account and all associated data (profile, cycle logs, notes, rewards history, redemptions, subscription records, device records and export records) are permanently erased from our systems. The grace period exists only so that an accidental or unauthorised deletion can be reversed by contacting us.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: in the app go to Settings and choose "Export my data". A copy is sent to your account email.
  • Correct: edit your profile and any log entry in the app at any time.
  • Erase your logs: in Settings choose "Reset all data" to permanently delete your logs, points and settings while keeping your account.
  • Delete your account: in Settings choose "Delete account", or follow the steps on our account deletion page. You do not need the app installed to request deletion.
  • Reminders: turn each reminder type on or off in Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, the EU or UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Spot it is not intended for children under 13, and the app does not allow a date of birth under 13 to be entered. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. If you use Spot it from another country, your information will be transferred to and stored in the United States. We rely on your consent, given when you create your account, for this transfer.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above, and where a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
